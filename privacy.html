Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker — Effective date: March 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Expense Tracker app ("we", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide the expense tracking service:

  • Google Account data: Your name and email address obtained via Google Sign-In for authentication purposes.
  • SMS data: Bank transaction SMS messages read directly from your device to automatically parse and categorise expenses. This data is processed locally and transmitted securely to our server for categorisation only.
  • Transaction data: Amounts, merchants, dates, and categories derived from SMS parsing, stored in your account on our server.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you securely via Google Sign-In.
  • To read bank SMS messages and extract transaction details automatically.
  • To display your expenses, categories, and spending summaries within the app.
  • To improve parsing accuracy for Indian bank SMS formats.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3. SMS Permission

The app requests READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS permissions to automatically detect and import bank transaction messages. This is the core feature of the app. SMS messages are:

  • Read only from your device.
  • Filtered to bank/financial messages only.
  • Transmitted securely over HTTPS to our server for parsing.
  • Never stored in raw form beyond what is needed to extract transaction data.
  • Never shared with any third party.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your transaction data is stored on a secured server (shivarya.dev) with access protected by JWT authentication. All communication between the app and server uses HTTPS (TLS). Passwords are not stored — authentication is handled entirely via Google OAuth.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may contact us to request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC): For authentication. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo (Expo Inc.): For app build and update infrastructure.

We do not use analytics SDKs, ad networks, or tracking frameworks.

7. Children's Privacy

Expense Tracker is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent for SMS access at any time via Android app permissions settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
