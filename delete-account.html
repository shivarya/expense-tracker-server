Delete Your Account

Expense Tracker — Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Expense Tracker account and all associated data at any time. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

⚠️ Deletion is permanent. All your transactions, categories, accounts, and profile data will be erased immediately.

What gets deleted

  • Your user profile (name, email)
  • All transaction history
  • Custom categories
  • Bank accounts and linked data
  • EMI tracking records
  • All SMS sync history

How to delete your account

1
Open the Expense Tracker app on your phone.
2
Tap the More tab at the bottom of the screen.
3
Scroll down to the Account section.
4
Tap Delete Account (red button).
5
Confirm the two-step prompt. Your account and all data will be deleted immediately.

Prefer to request by email?

If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request to:
ash@shivarya.dev

Include the email address associated with your account. We will process the request within 7 business days.

After deletion

  • You will be signed out immediately.
  • Your data is deleted from our servers with no backup retained.
  • You can create a new account at any time using Google Sign-In.
