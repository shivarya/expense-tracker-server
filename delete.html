Delete Your Account & Data

Expense Tracker  ·  Contact: shivarya.dev@gmail.com

Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2 — By email

Email shivarya.dev@gmail.com from your sign-up address with subject "Delete my account". After verifying ownership we will delete your data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Profile (name, email, Google id, picture), stored Gmail token, all transactions, accounts, categories, investments (stocks, mutual funds, fixed deposits, PF/NPS/PPF), EMIs, statement uploads, saved statement passwords, transaction groups, category-learning rules, sync logs, and subscription records.

What is retained

Nothing tied to your identity. Google retains billing records per its own policies (we do not store payment details).

Revoke access without deleting

Disconnect Gmail in the app (More → Gmail Auto-Sync → Disconnect) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions; revoke SMS permission in Android Settings → Apps → Expense Tracker → Permissions.
